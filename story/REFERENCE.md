# The Unseen Sun — reference

Everyone who appears, everywhere it happens, and every event the film is built
from. (*Loka Kalyāṇam* is the film's theme — the welfare of the world — and not
a Telugu translation of the English title; `THE_STORY.md` has the titling note
and four Telugu alternatives.) This is the naming authority for the project: when a scene, a shot or a
prompt names a person or a place, it is spelled the way it is spelled here.

The Telugu column is the name as the board shows it when the language is
switched; it matches `data/te/terms.json`.

Event codes (`E01`…`E08`) are the ones in `story/events/` and in every scene's
`src` field. Scene codes (`S-438`) are deliberately unordered and live only in
`data/scenes.json`.

## 3. Story events

Eight events, 115 scenes, about three hours and twenty minutes of material.
On the board they can be ordered in any way; these descriptions are what each
one *is*, not where it falls.

### E01 — Kṣīra Sāgara Manthanam, the churning of the Ocean of Milk
**క్షీరసాగర మథనం** · 19 scenes · ≈ 34 min

A sage's gift is trampled by a careless king of the gods, and heaven loses its
light. To win back what they lost, the gods must make an alliance with their
enemies and churn the ocean together. The labour brings up poison before it
brings up nectar, and when the nectar comes the alliance breaks over it. It is
the film's oldest story and its pattern: the Lord is never in the room, the
prize is never held by the one who seizes it, and the goddess chooses the one
who did not ask.

- *The garland and the curse* — Indra drapes a sage's gift on his elephant.
- *The fall of the Devas* — colour drains from heaven; the gods run.
- *The petition* — Brahmā takes them to the shore, and a voice answers.
- *The humiliating alliance* — Indra bows to Bali.
- *Mandara sinks, Kūrma lifts it* — a vast back rises under the mountain.
- *The great churning* — endurance as action; nobody sees what holds it up.
- *Hālāhala* — the poison comes first; a fisher child stays at the water.
- *Śiva drinks it* — Pārvatī's hand at his throat.
- *The treasures* — a cow, a horse, an elephant, a jewel, a tree.
- *Lakṣmī chooses* — she passes every claimant.
- *The Asuras refused* — and given wine instead.
- *The pot seized* — the circle that held for the whole churning breaks.
- *Mohinī* — the nectar is served to one line.
- *Rāhu exposed* — a head that outlives its body.
- *The Deva–Asura war* — heaven is taken back.
- *Śiva before Mohinī* — even the wise are undone by wonder.
- *The fisher family* — the water is theirs again, and they never know why.
- *The mural* and *the bard's recital* — how all of this reaches Rukmini.

### E02 — Bhūbhāram, the Earth's burden
**భూభారం** · 9 scenes · ≈ 14 min

Armies march and the ground gives under them. The Earth, in the form of a cow
who cannot stand, goes to Brahmā; Brahmā goes to the shore; a voice from the
sky assigns the births that will answer her. Mathurā becomes the Yadu capital,
and a farmer somewhere finds his field too heavy to work. This is why Krishna
is born, and the film holds it back until after we have seen him.

- *The march of the burden* · *The Earth goes to Brahmā* · *The shore and the hymn*
- *The voice from the sky* · *The births are assigned* · *Mathurā becomes the capital*
- *The farmer and his cow* · *The cow that will not move* · *"Asked for, before he was born"*

### E03 — Jarasandha's attacks on Mathurā
**జరాసంధుని దాడులు** · 15 scenes · ≈ 28 min

Kaṁsa's widows go home to Magadha and their father answers with twenty-three
divisions at Mathurā's gate. He is beaten, captured and let go — and comes
back, seventeen times. On the last of them Krishna and Balarama are chased up
a hill on foot and the hill is set alight; Jarasandha rides home believing he
has won. In Kuṇḍina, Rukmī tells the story as proof of a great king's
persistence, and Rukmini hears a different story inside it.

- *Jarasandha seized and freed* · *The near-penance* · *Seventeen times* · *The return and the spoils*
- *The chase on foot* · *The burning hill* · *The leap and the false victory*
- *Rukmī admires Jarasandha* · *"Seventeen times"* — the tally as Kuṇḍina hears it

### E04 — Dvārakā rises from the sea
**ద్వారకా నిర్మాణం** · 10 scenes · ≈ 16 min

Thirty million soldiers arrive at the other gate while Jarasandha is still at
the first. Rather than fight both, Krishna moves the city: a new one is raised
out of the water, a whole people wake in it, and he walks out of its gate
alone and unarmed to deal with what is left behind. Sailors carry the tale to
a ferry near Kuṇḍina, and Rukmini tries to imagine a city on water.

### E05 — Mucukunda and the man on foot
**ముచుకుంద గుహ** · 16 scenes · ≈ 27 min

Kālayavana comes for a man he has only heard described. He finds him unarmed
and walking, and follows him — on foot, alone, for miles — into a cave where
an old king has been sleeping since the gods' war, with a boon that whoever
wakes him burns. Kālayavana wakes him. Mucukunda sees the one who led him
there, asks for nothing, and walks north. The army outside is destroyed; a
goatherd sees the smoke and makes a story of it.

### E06 — Rukmiṇī Kalyāṇam, the letter and the abduction
**రుక్మిణీ హరణం** · 31 scenes · ≈ 60 min

The spine of the film. Rukmini has heard of Krishna for years; her brother

guard, prays, and at the door sees his face for the first time. He lifts her
into the chariot and takes her out through a ring of kings. The pursuit is
broken; her brother swears an oath, loses the duel, and is shaved and exiled
rather than killed, at her asking.

## 4. Spelling

The scene data is the authority, and this document follows it. Two names are
written without their diacritics there, and so here: **Balarama** (not
Balarāma) and **Jarasandha** (not Jarāsandha). Everything else keeps its
marks: Rukmī, Bhīṣmaka, Śiśupāla, Kālayavana, Kuṇḍina, Dvārakā, Mathurā,
Ambikā, Lakṣmī, Śiva, Mucukunda, Kṣīra Sāgara.

Rukmini is written without the final long mark — **Rukmini**, not Rukmiṇī —
because that is how she is named everywhere in the data and on the board. Her
brother keeps his: **Rukmī**. The two are one letter apart on purpose; when
writing a prompt or a shot, check which one you mean.

## 5. The rules the names serve

- **Krishna is not seen before the abduction.** In every scene before
  `E06-18`, he is *Not present*, *Spoken of*, *Shadow or sound*, *Hands*,
  *Effect*, or a *Reflection in the witness's face*. The board enforces this.
- **Nothing follows the final episode.** E07 ends every cut of the film.
- **Rukmini is the protagonist.** Everything in E01–E05 exists because it
  reaches her: a mural, a bard, a sailor, a goatherd, a tally of sieges.
